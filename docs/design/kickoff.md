# Product Alignment & Design Kickoff

## Vision Alignment Summary
- Build a conversational sports intelligence assistant blending real-time odds, analytics, and guided betting workflows.
- Deliver Bloomberg-style dense information with a chat-first interface optimized for mobile responsiveness.

## Primary User Journeys
1. **Onboarding & Personalization**
   - Sign in with Supabase Auth, choose bettor profile, connect bankroll sources.
   - Walkthrough highlights tone toggle, bankroll tracker, and edge alerts subscription.
2. **Daily Home Hub**
   - Chat-based summary of bankroll status, recent bets, and notable market movements.
   - Toggle concise/engaging tone to adjust responses.
3. **Bet & Bankroll Tracking**
   - Input bets via manual form or natural language parsing.
   - Review ROI analytics, streaks, and behavioral tags in a dedicated bankroll view.
4. **Line & Expected Value Scanner**
   - Aggregate odds by market, surface differential edges, and allow quick bet logging.
   - Provide inline explanations and context-specific advice.
5. **Edge Alerts & Creator Feed**
   - Subscribe to creators, receive push-style alerts in chat when thresholds trigger.
   - Access accountability reports and line movement explanations.

## UX Artifacts to Produce in Figma MCP
- Mobile-first wireframes for chat hub, bankroll tracker, EV scanner, and edge cards.
- Interaction flows illustrating onboarding, bet logging, and alert drilldowns.
- Component library for chat bubbles, edge card layouts, analytics widgets, and alert badges.

## Design Notes
- Maintain high information density without sacrificing readability; favor tabs, accordions, and progressive disclosure.
- Ensure color palette supports quick scanning of win/loss, edge magnitude, and confidence levels.
- Reserve space for future moat features like cross-market intelligence and psychology insights.
